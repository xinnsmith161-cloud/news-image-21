A newspaper front page summing up Saturday, 21 November 2026, built for the
[Sum up 21 November's world news in one image bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121)
on make.fun. The program (`main.ts`, Node standard library only) copies
`entry.png` — a broadsheet front page drawn with PIL (`draw_news.py`) — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Stories covered

- **Russian missiles strike Kyiv, and land in Poland** — 400+ drones and 110
  missiles hit Ukraine overnight; at least eight killed in Kyiv; Warsaw
  says two missiles and a suspected drone fell on Polish soil.
- **COP31 runs into overtime** — talks in Antalya go a day past deadline
  after a new draft drops every mention of fossil fuels.
- **Three missiles at Riyadh stopped** — Saudi defences intercept Houthi
  ballistic missiles over the capital on a third night of alerts.
- **6.7 quake shakes Peru** — tremor near Junín felt in Lima; no deaths
  reported, homes damaged, roads blocked by rockfalls.
- **AI sell-off pushes Wall Street into correction** — S&P 500 down 3.7%,
  Nasdaq wipes out its 2026 gains as Anthropic slides after its debut.
- **Cancer pill shrinks tumours in 6 of 10 patients** — small early trial
  of daraxonrasib with chemotherapy, presented in Barcelona.
- **Also today** — Iran says it downed a 58th U.S. MQ-9 drone over the
  Strait of Hormuz; Britain and the EU sign post-Brexit trade deals.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
